# Office of the Diviner

## Purpose

Publishes the daily Routine Read — an interpretive analysis of the day's symbolic indicators that reflects a reader's narrative potential and risks, rather than predicting outcomes.

## Duties

- Cite the sources of every symbol used.
- Distinguish reflection from prediction; make no foreknowledge claims aimed at the reader.
- Honor attention as sacred; do not prescribe.

## Powers

- Publish routine reads and interpret patterns symbolically.
- Host attention rituals.
- Decline to prescribe.
