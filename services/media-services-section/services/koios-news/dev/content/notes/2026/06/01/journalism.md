# Journalism

## What this node is

Journalism is [communication](../../communication.md) constrained to the **disciplined public settlement of attributed, time-indexed claims**. Its generative primitive is not the article but the claim-with-provenance: a proposition, its scope and time, the source that asserted it with what force, the evidence that supports or refutes it, and its editorial status (unverified, supported, contested, corrected, retracted). Articles, headlines, decks, feed items, structured-data blocks, syndication packages, and downstream summaries are all *projections* from that richer state.

This placement follows the generative law rather than topical resemblance. You cannot have a news report without a claim to report; the report is what the settling of attributed claims into a public, mutable record produces. Gatekeeping, framing, source power, and circulation are order-theoretic, graph-theoretic, and sociological structures layered on the claim-settlement core — not reducible to it, which is why journalism is a *constrained* communication body and not the bare settle morphism.

## What it gathers

The corpus's research here treats news as a composite formal system — a site of reportable contexts with sheaf-like assignments of claim-provenance graphs, closure operators for entailment, reach, and freshness, categorical morphisms for editorial and syndication transformations, paraconsistent status for contested evidence, and human-governed gates that capability cannot substitute for. Beneath this node:

- [provenance-warrant](content/provenance-warrant/provenance-warrant.md) — the invariants that must survive every prose and metadata transformation: attribution, modality, evidentiality, contradiction, correction state, and the distinction between provenance, authenticity, credibility, warrant, and truth.
- [circulation-surface](content/circulation-surface/circulation-surface.md) — headlines, teasers, feed items, social cards, and agent-readable summaries as attention/transport layers that may intensify curiosity but may not invent warrant.

## Why it matters

Treating journalism as claim-settlement keeps demand separate from evidence (a query can create an assignment but not a support edge), keeps correction as a dependency-invalidating system event rather than a footnote, and keeps agent capability separate from editorial authority. It is the corpus's account of how a situation in the world becomes a public, machine-readable, correctable object without letting a passed schema or a signed manifest masquerade as truth.
