- **Keyword/source discovery is live** (`src/researching/sourcing/discovery.mts`). A pure reading over the owned wire stores (on disk, no server) and the published corpus produces: coverage gaps (seed terms the intake supports but the corpus barely covers), candidate keywords (frequent multi-word intake phrases no seed names, after an RSS-boilerplate stoplist), candidate sources (domains the bibliography cites but no wire feeds), and an optional Google Trends signal (keyless daily-RSS probe, obstruction-as-value, matched against seed vocabulary so off-beat trends are dropped). - **Citation closure reduces the highest editorial friction.** `@emsenn/content/topology/copyedit` now has `linkSources`, the faithfulness-safe dual of the citations gate: a witnessed source's exact title (≥4 words) appearing verbatim and unlinked in open prose gets its first occurrence wrapped as `[title](url)` — invents nothing, links only witnessed URLs, title verbatim (satisfies title-fidelity too), caps supporting at two, idempotent. The farm `defaultCompose` threads `intakeSources = [story, ...context]` (story first, always linked). A draft that names sources without linking them now clears citations with no second LLM call.
