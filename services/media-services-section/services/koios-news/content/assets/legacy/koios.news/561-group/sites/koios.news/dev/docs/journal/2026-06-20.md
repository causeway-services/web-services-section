# One tick, then sharpening the parts that hurt

## What I did, in order

I ran Koios through one real cycle and then fixed the three friction points the
run exposed — keyword selection, citation drafting, and machine legibility.

### 1. One piece went live

I started the Koios-owned cable (`dist/cable-server.mjs`, port 18809), ingested
the wires (politics and finance most active today), and ran `source:cycle`
against the real NIM provider. The farm assigned `de-dollarization of commodity
trade` (collapse-finance) and drafted from a Guardian Heathrow third-runway
story plus a Hormuz-mines story.

The candidate (`heathrow-s-third-runway-a-62-5bn-miscalculation-in`) cleared all
gates but two: the SEO term sat in no `##` subheader, and the meta description
ran 153 chars. The term/story pairing was the now-familiar misalignment — the
de-dollarization term shoehorned onto a runway story — but the body carried the
de-dollarization thread honestly through its logistics section, so I retermed
that section's heading to name it and trimmed the description. Standards review
went `publishable: true` with no findings; I published through
`publishManuscript`. It is live on `/`, `/feed.json`, and `/newswire-audit.json`.

### 2. Keyword and source discovery (new capability)

The standing friction: demand only ranks the *existing* seed list, Search
Console still 403s, and nothing proposes new coverage. I added
`src/researching/sourcing/discovery.mts` — a pure reading over two owned
substrates (the wire stores on disk and the published corpus), so it runs with
no server and no credentials:

- **coverage gaps** — seed terms the intake strongly supports but the corpus
  barely covers (e.g. today: `AI policy and state capacity`, intake support 141,
  coverage 0);
- **candidate keywords** — frequent multi-word intake phrases no seed names
  (`large language models`, `clean energy`, `federal reserve`, …), after an
  RSS-boilerplate stoplist;
- **candidate sources** — domains the bibliography keeps citing but no wire
  feeds (`reuters.com`, `apnews.com`, `phys.org`), i.e. sources worth wiring;
- **trends** — a keyless Google Trends daily-RSS probe folded as
  obstruction-or-value, matched against seed vocabulary so off-beat celebrity/
  sport trends are dropped rather than mislabelled as topics.

Surfaces: `pnpm run discover` (CLI), `GET /operators/research/discovery.json`
(`?trends=1` to fold trends), OpenAPI `readKoiosKeywordDiscovery`, MCP
`read_keyword_discovery`, and a workbench policy pointer. Discovery **proposes**;
it never writes the seed list or wire config — demand creates assignments, not
evidence.

### 3. Citation closure (friction reduction in the cycle)

The highest editorial friction is the citations gate: the single compose call
often names sources in prose without linking them, so the draft is withheld and
a human inserts the links by hand. I added `linkSources` to
`@emsenn/content/topology/copyedit` — the faithfulness-safe dual of the
citations gate. When a witnessed source's exact title (≥ 4 words) appears
verbatim in open prose and unlinked, the first occurrence is wrapped as
`[title](url)`. It invents nothing, links only witnessed URLs, uses the title
verbatim (so it also satisfies title-fidelity), caps supporting links at two,
and is idempotent (the wrapped text becomes a protected link span). The farm's
`defaultCompose` now threads `intakeSources = [story, ...context]`, story-first
so the subject is always linked. Smoke-checked: a previously-failing draft now
clears the citations gate with zero extra LLM calls; second pass makes no edits;
all output URLs are witnessed.

### 4. Coverage knowledge graph (machine-readable richness, no visual change)

Feeds say which articles exist; nothing said what the publication *covers*. I
added `/coverage.json` — a schema.org `DefinedTermSet` projecting the published
corpus into a controlled vocabulary of SEO terms, beats, sections, and tags,
each `DefinedTerm` linked to the `NewsArticle`s covering it (229 terms over 51
articles). It is advertised in `/syndicating.json`. Pure linked data for
crawlers/agents; the human site renders nothing new — verified the existing
article HTML (`/posts/hello-world`) is byte-identical before/after and the
homepage diff is empty.

## Verification

- `@emsenn/content`, `@emsenn/content-production`, `@emsenn/koios.news` all build
  and typecheck.
- `discover` CLI returns 27 coverage gaps, 22 candidate keywords, 6 candidate
  sources; trends probe reaches Google and folds gracefully.
- newsroom `read_keyword_discovery` MCP tool + `/operators/research/discovery.json`
  + OpenAPI all live (probed on 18803).
- `/coverage.json` returns the DefinedTermSet (probed on 18802); syndication
  advertises it; existing pages byte-identical.
- `linkSources` smoke test: links story + support, idempotent, faithful,
  citations gate flips to pass.

## What's still open

- Discovery proposes; promoting a candidate keyword into the seed config or a
  source into the wire is still a human edit. That boundary is deliberate, but a
  guarded "promote candidate → assignment" action is the natural next step.
- The term/story misalignment that needed hand-reterming this cycle is unfixed
  at the source: the support-walk admits a term on beat-level support, then
  `selectStory` can pick a story that fits the beat but not the term. A
  per-candidate alignment score (story↔term distinctive-word overlap) folded
  into the source-cycle repairs would surface this before publication.
- The published Heathrow post is live by serving but not yet committed in the
  `content/` submodule, and the session's `src/` capability work is uncommitted
  in this repo.
