AcuRite, a smart weather-monitoring device vendor, has delayed its plans to force users onto a new companion app, AcuRite NOW, due to concerns over data center water use and the app's shortcomings.
The transition from My AcuRite to AcuRite NOW, which was previously set for May 30, "has raised serious questions and concerns among long-time users," according to Jeff Bovee, AcuRite's VP of product development.
This delay highlights the fragility of IoT systems, which rely on complex networks and data centers with significant water use.

## The Complexity of IoT Systems and Data Center Water Use The AcuRite app transition issue is not an isolated event, but a symptom of a larger problem.
The complexity of IoT systems, which rely on multiple components and data centers, makes them vulnerable to disruptions.
For example, a single data center can use millions of gallons of water per day, straining local resources and exacerbating drought conditions.
This is particularly concerning in regions where water scarcity is already a significant issue.
According to [AcuRite admits new app falls short, delays old app’s May shutdown to fix problems](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/06/iot-gadget-firm-acurite-delays-forced-app-migration-due-to-new-apps-shortfalls/), the company has acknowledged the shortcomings of its new app, which might be related to the strain on data centers and their water use.
The affected users will likely face significant challenges in adapting to the new app, and the delay might provide an opportunity for AcuRite to address these concerns and develop a more robust and sustainable solution.
