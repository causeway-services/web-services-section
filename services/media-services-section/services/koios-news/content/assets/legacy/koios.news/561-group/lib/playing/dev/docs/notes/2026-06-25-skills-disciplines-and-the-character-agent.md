# Skills, Disciplines, and the Character-Agent

This register connects the performance-theory work in `@emsenn/playing` (affect, genre, Caillois, dramaturgy, persona/mask) to the corpus's OWN prior research on character/persona-as-agent — and finds they are the same thesis, which tells us what skills and disciplines must be on this substrate.

## The prior research (found)

- `lib/561-group/.../memory/character-agent-spec-distillation.md` — the 561 Group EXPLICITLY rejected the industry "character-agent = governed state machine" framing (IdentitySpec + PersonaPolicy + BehaviorPolicy + VoicePolicy + MemorySystem + … + a 6-level maturity model). emsenn: "you keep importing abstractions"; the substrate (predication-categorical) is the deeper ground; a character "becomes better" by substrate-quiescence (wičozaní), NOT architectural sophistication.
  Persona was migrated INTO character as a substrate entity (`migrate-persona-to-character-term`).
- The `SKILL.md` forest (`lib/561-group/.../working/.../<gerund>/SKILL.md`) — a skill is a typed 𝒞-morphism in the predication category: `gerund`/`agent-noun`/`imperative`, an `originating` (domain) and `terminating` (codomain) object, a `closure-position ∈ {process, procedure, derivation, inquiry}` (the skillful-work quartet), and a specialization tree by name-grammar.
  Characters live in working DOMAINS (divining, engineering, performing, governing, …) — the disciplines.

## The overlap (why :o)

The industry model stacks policies BEHIND the agent's face and climbs a maturity ladder.
The corpus rejects this; the ludic `performativity` reading (persona.mts) is the refutation made theorem: there is no policy-stack behind the agent — the character IS the union of its chronicles ("a design is the union of its chronicles", Girard §4.7); the only thing that looks like an "essence behind the face" is the bi-orthogonal closure, an EFFECT of the audience's counter-plays, not a prior cause.
"Becomes better = quiescence" is "the cut converges / the design goes material," not "add a policy module."
The performance-theory persona layer and the corpus's character-as-agent thesis are one claim.

## What skills and disciplines are, on the substrate

- **Discipline = a behaviour.** A working domain (a practice) is the bi-orthogonal closure of its exemplary plays — the magic-circle'd set of plays that COUNT in the practice.
  A character practices a discipline iff its design lies in that behaviour.
  (Reuses playing's `biorthogonalClosure`; gaming's `magic-circle`/`lusory-attitude` are the gaming-side framing of the same boundary.)
- **Skill = a typed reusable design.** A strategy with a domain→codomain and a `closure-position` from the skillful-work quartet, deployable across plays (it COMPOSES via the cut).
  MASTERED in a discipline iff it is MATERIAL there (incarnated, no ballast — no wasted commitment) and ADMITTED (its design lies in the discipline's behaviour).
  (Reuses `incarnationOf`/`materialJudgment`/`orthogonal`.)
- **The skillful-work quartet** inquiry/procedure/derivation/process = the σ(meaning)/Δ(execution) nuclei × phase (opening/settled): inquiry = σ·open (ask), derivation = σ·settled (prove), procedure = Δ·open (the method), process = Δ·settled (the run).
  Another face of the recurring FOUR.
- **Character = personas + skills + disciplines + its chronicles.** An agent that wears masks (delocations), commands skills (material typed designs), practices disciplines (behaviours), and IS the sedimented union of its plays.
  Not a state machine; a substrate agent.
  (gaming already has `strategizing/character`; the character bundle should relate to it, not duplicate it.)
- **Identity drift is now proof-carrying.** `identity(character).cohomology` normalizes the drift-locus obstruction as `cohomologyTerms.settlingFirst`: one generator per mask-gluing contradiction, with rank equal to `identity(character).drift`.
